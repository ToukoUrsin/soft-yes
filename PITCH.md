# Soft Yes, the 3 minute pitch

About 300 spoken words. That is 2:05 of talking and leaves 55 seconds for the clicks. The mic is cut at 3:00.

Laptop open on https://soft-yes.fly.dev in a private window. Read the numbers off the screen when you say them: the totals grow as people use the site.

**0:00** Every founder here has heard it. "We're in if you find a lead." It sounds like a yes. It isn't one until someone leads.

**0:10** Soft Yes finds that lead. It looks at the rounds your investors already joined and who led them. Whoever led has already done a deal with your investor, so your investor can introduce you.

**0:22** *Click the example "Tesi + Innovestor".*
Say Tesi and Innovestor told us that. In Cala's graph, 19 investors led their rounds. First is Lifeline Ventures. Cala has it leading 8 rounds in the last two years, one of them Verda's, with Tesi in it. Here's the sentence on the page that says so.

**0:50** *Point at the two struck lines under it.*
Now the part we didn't expect. Cala says Lifeline led two more rounds with Tesi. We opened the page Cala cites for each. Measur's own blog says VentureFriends led. Seaber's page names someone else too. Tell Tesi that Lifeline led Measur and you've lost them. Tesi was in that round. So we only count a lead when the page says it.

**1:18** *Click "Copy message".*
The ask to Tesi is already written. Every name and number in it is one you just saw on this page.

**1:30** *Open "Page check" in the top bar.*
We've checked over 700 leads this way. The page confirms more than 500. 18 it contradicts, and you can read the sentence for each. Check us. *Type "Lovable".* Menlo led the Series C, in Lovable's own words.

**1:55** Cala team: those 18 are in one file, each with the sentence that contradicts it. It's yours.

**2:05** Does it work? We took 41 old rounds, hid who led, and gave it two investors who joined. It had the real lead 11 times. Guessing gets 5.

**2:22** *Back to the result.*
Slush is next week, and 7 of these leads are on the attendee list. So next time someone says "find a lead", you have a name. It's live at soft-yes.fly.dev. Try it with an investor you know.

## If they ask

- **Why Cala, why not web search?** Search gives you pages. This is a question about who was in which round, and Cala answers it in one call per investor, with the page behind each fact.
- **Cala was wrong 18 times. Is that a problem for you?** It's why this works. Every fact comes with its page, so we can check it.
- **What about the other 200 claims?** For 120 the page names no lead, and 77 pages wouldn't open. We show both and never count them as confirmed.
- **Is Measur the same round?** Yes. It's the page Cala cites for that round, and it names Lifeline and Tesi as joining it.
- **Two of three were wrong. Why is Lifeline still first?** It leads a lot, and the Verda round holds on the page. We picked this example because it shows the check. Overall it's 18 in 700.
- **Are all 8 confirmed on the page?** The 8 is Cala's count. We open the page for every round it shares with your investors, because that's what goes in the intro.
- **Who reads the page?** Code, no model. It looks for the investor's name next to "led by" in the page's own sentence and shows you that sentence. We went through the 18 by hand.
- **What if Cala knows few rounds for my investor?** It says so and gives a short list with no first pick. Add a second investor: that's the 13 of 57.
- **And from one investor?** Too close to chance in our test (8 of 144). That's why the form asks for two.
- **What is "at random"?** An investor who led some round that same year.
- **Where is the Slush list from?** Slush's own attendee list, the one every ticket holder sees.
- **Who uses it tomorrow?** A founder with one soft yes and Slush next week. Their assistant can call it too, it's an MCP server.
- **What's next?** The investor side. A fund types its own name and sees who it follows.
