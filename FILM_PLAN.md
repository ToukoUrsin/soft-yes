## The story in 3 lines
- **Who:** a founder raising a round.
- **The pain:** an investor says "we're in, if you find a lead". That is not a yes yet. In Cala, Tesi led only 2 of its 18 rounds, so the lead has to come from someone else.
- **The turn:** Soft Yes finds who led the rounds your investors joined, then opens the page behind every "led". Cala says Lifeline Ventures led 3 rounds next to Tesi. The pages confirm 1 and name another lead for 2.

**The one moment a judge retells:** "Cala said this fund led three rounds. The tool opened the pages Cala cites, struck two of them out and showed the sentence that names who really led. Then it wrote the intro email from what was left."
