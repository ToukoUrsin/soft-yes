Soft Yes in a chat
The site

They said yes, if you find a lead. Ask in your own chat.

Soft Yes is a connector for your assistant. It walks Cala’s funding graph from the investors who said yes, checks every “led” against the page Cala cites, and draws it in the chat. Pick a question to see the card.

A stand-in chat with no language model behind it. What you send goes straight to Soft Yes, and every fact in the card is Cala’s.

Add Soft Yes to your assistant

It works in any assistant that takes a custom connector (MCP). With your mail connected too, it finds the soft yeses itself and saves the ask as a draft.

  1. Open your assistant’s connector settings and add a custom connector.
  2. Paste the address above. No sign-in.
  3. Ask: “Who said they’re in once we have a lead? Find who could lead.”
